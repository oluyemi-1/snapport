Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

Snapport is a mobile app published by Karitec Limited ("we", "us"). It helps you create passport, visa, and ID photos that meet official document specifications.

This policy explains what data Snapport handles, how it's processed, and what your rights are. Plain language only.

The short version

  • Snapport does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers. We don't operate a backend for Snapport.
  • All photo capture, face detection, background removal, and image processing happens on your device. Your photos never leave your phone unless you share them.
  • No analytics. No third-party tracking. No advertising.
  • No account required. Snapport does not ask you to sign up or sign in.

What Snapport accesses on your device

DataWhyWhere it goes
CameraTo take a passport photo when you choose "Take photo".Stays on device. Used only for the active session.
Photo Library (read)To let you pick an existing photo when you choose "Upload from gallery".Stays on device. Used only for the active session.
Photo Library (add)To save the finished passport photo back to your gallery if you choose to.Stays on device. Saved by you, controlled by you.
Temporary storageTo hold the in-progress photo while we process it.Cleared automatically by your operating system.

You grant or deny each of these permissions through the standard system prompt. You can revoke them at any time in your device's Settings.

What Snapport does NOT collect

Snapport does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email, phone number, address, or any other contact information
  • Your location
  • Your photos (the ones you capture or upload), in any form, on any server
  • Your face data, biometric identifiers, or any derivative
  • Device identifiers used for advertising or tracking
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports linked to your identity
  • Browsing or search history

On-device face detection

Snapport uses Google ML Kit's on-device face detection to check that your photo meets the requirements (correct head size, eye line, single face visible, etc.). The model runs entirely on your device. No image data is sent to Google or anyone else as part of this check.

Sharing the finished photo

When you choose to save your finished photo to your Photo Library, or to share it via the share sheet to another app (email, messaging, the Nigeria Immigration Service upload portal, etc.), you are doing that yourself. Snapport does not transmit your photo on your behalf to any third party.

Children

Snapport is rated 4+ on the App Store and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children — we don't collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Snapport handles data, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
